documents submitted to the CA by the startup
The startup must submit the following documents to the CA to obtain a legal document of the equity and deal with the investor:
Certificate of Incorporation: Issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs on registration as a Private Limited Company, LLP or One Person Company. It carries the Corporate Identity Number and is the document every bank, investor, and landlord will ask for first. 
1
Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Articles of Association (AoA): These are the constitutional rulebooks of your Private Limited Company under the Companies Act, 2013. They dictate what your business is legally allowed to do, and the internal rules of management. 
1
Shareholders Agreement: Covers equity splits, drag-along rights, and exit terms. 
1
Founders Agreement: Outlines roles, equity splits, decision-making powers, and capital contributions before the company is officially registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). 
1
ESOP Plan: Ensures that the company's shares are allocated to employees as per the Employees' State Insurance and Pension Scheme (ESOP). 
1
IP Assignment Deeds: Assign every line of code, design, and document created by a founder, including work done before incorporation, to the company. 
1
Board Minutes: Document the decisions made by the board of directors. 
1

These documents are essential for ensuring that the startup is legally compliant and that the equity and deal with the investor are properly documented.
